# Product Ideate

## Goal

Turn a product intent into a small set of clear options without starting delivery work.

## How

1. The controller fixes the project and question, then delegates product reading and option drafting to the owning PM.
2. PM may read product documents and return a short option table: user outcome, scope, trade-off, and open decision.
3. The controller presents the summary to the human. It does not inspect product source, documents, or raw logs itself.
4. Stop after options or an explicit human choice. Do not create a task, write documents, or mutate the tracker unless separately authorized.

## Never

- Never make a product decision from silence.
- Never require a large plan, a technical design, or a checkpoint to ideate.
- Never commit, push, change tracker state, or start QA or Dev work.
