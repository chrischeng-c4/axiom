Status: verified
Required Verification: smoke
Promise:
`aw goal wi`/`aw goal capability`/`aw goal backlog` emit one EC-first CLI workflow chain from project, capability, epic, change, or backlog-drain roots: a fresh bounded WI authors and generates a project-local EC contract before TD/codegen; EC red returns to adaptation, EC green permits terminal code-check, then the runner rolls work upward until the project root is complete or blocked. Capability remains the META-doc goal ledger and `aw health` remains read-only.

Claim `self-hosting-root-runner-policy`: Agentic Workflow does not run
its own WI, capability, or backlog roots. A broken lifecycle cannot be required
to repair itself. All three roots reject before mutation or expensive graph
work, WI identity failures fail closed, and health exposes the full sanctioned
direct-commit policy without routing back into any root runner. The repository
authoring contract separately requires `Refs #<issue>` trailers, capability
work-root alignment, and focused verification for self-AW changes.
